A letter to you

Dear Sir,

Whenever I hear the words 'Teacher's Day', you are the first teacher who comes to my mind.

When I look back at my high-school days, I realize that you gave me much more than classroom lessons. You taught me, guided me, encouraged me, and most importantly, you helped build confidence in me.

I learned many things from you, and some of those lessons have stayed with me long after I left school.

And then there are the little memories — the handmade Teacher's Day greeting, the pen you gave me, the chocolate and little Vinayaka idol I gave you, the scooty ride, and so many other moments.

They may have been ordinary moments to you, but they became special memories for me.

I may not have said it enough when I was your student, but today I want you to know how grateful I am.

Thank you for believing in me.

Thank you for teaching me.

Thank you for guiding me.

And thank you for being a teacher I will always remember.

I feel truly lucky that I got the opportunity to be your student.

Happy Teacher's Day, Sir.

With heartfelt gratitude,

Your student, Spoorthy
